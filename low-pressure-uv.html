(NCL Series)

Download Product Catalog
The Neotec UV NCL Series is a Low-Pressure High-Output (LPHO) Amalgam UV disinfection system designed for small-to-medium scale applications including drinking water, wastewater, swimming pools, and aquaculture.

The reactor and control panel feature a Plug-and-Play design, eliminating the need for separate electrical wiring work during installation.

Key Features

The Neotec UV NCL Series utilizes CFD-modeled design and energy-efficient LPHO lamp technology for reliable, cost-effective disinfection.
‍
• Equipped with an automated, motor-driven wiper mechanism and user-friendly control interfaces for simple operation and minimal maintenance.
• High-efficiency Low-Pressure High-Output (LPHO) lamps deliver extended lamp life and significantly reduce operational and maintenance costs.
• Compact design with a small footprint ensures quick, easy installation in space-constrained facilities.
• Photolysis Models Available: Customized options engineered specifically for advanced oxidation and photolysis applications.
