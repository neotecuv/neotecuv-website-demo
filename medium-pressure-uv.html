MP UV Reactor

(NCM Series)

Download Product Catalog
The Neotec UV NCM Series is a Medium-Pressure (MP) UV disinfection system for drinking water, wastewater, swimming pools, and aquaculture, with internationally validated performance.

Each lamp is individually monitored, with automatic output adjustment based on flow rate and UVT.
‍
Quartz sleeves are maintained by a mechanical wiper, with an optional Bubble Clean system using microbubbles and circulation.

Key Features

The Neotec UV NCM Series utilizes CFD-RED analysis, and advanced lamp control for reliable performance.
‍
• Equipped with various functions such as an automatic cleaning system, UV monitoring, energy-saving operating mode, remote control function, and system monitoring device.
• Drinking Water Safety: Certified to NSF 61 & 372
• 25-100% lamp output for varying water quality and flow conditions
• High-power-factor ballasts used to lower energy consumption

Neotec UV NCM-I: Drinking Water and Wastewater Treatment
Neotec UV NCM-US: Swimming Pools, Spas and Recreational Water
Lamp Ouput Compensation System
Individual sensors detect lamp failure or reduced output and notify the control panel. The system compensates by increasing the output of surrounding lamps.
