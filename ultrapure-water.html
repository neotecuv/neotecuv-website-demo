Ultrapure Water

The Neotec UV NCL-T Series is a Vacuum Ultraviolet (VUV) system designed for TOC reduction in ultrapure water (UPW) treatment processes.

It utilizes high-output amalgam VUV lamps and CFD-based reactor optimization to achieve efficient organic compound oxidation, stable water quality, and reduced operating costs.

Available in standard and modular configurations, the system can be customized to accommodate various installation conditions and operational requirements.

Vacuum Ultraviolet Process

When exposed to Vacuum Ultraviolet (VUV) light at wavelengths below 200 nm, water molecules undergo photolysis, generating hydroxyl radicals (·OH). These highly reactive radicals break down organic compounds, reducing total organic carbon (TOC) levels.

VUV photolysis generates high concentrations of hydroxyl radicals (·OH) directly in water without the need for chemicals that could interfere with Ultrapure Water (UPW) treatment processes.
How VUV Works
1. VUV light irradiates water molecules.
2.  Photolysis generates hydroxyl radicals (·OH) without added chemicals.
3. Hydroxyl radicals oxidize organic compounds, reducing TOC levels.

Key Features

1) High-Efficiency Amalgam VUV Lamp Technology
The NCL-T Series features high-output amalgam lamps with a lifespan of up to 16,000 hours and power input of 300–330 W, requiring fewer lamps for efficient operation.
2) CFD-Based Reactor Optimization Design
CFD-based reactor optimization, such as lamp arrangement and baffle design, improves flow uniformity and enhances TOC removal efficiency in UPW processes.
3) Ozone Leakage Prevention & Lamp Protection
The specialized lamp socket provides enhanced sealing and vibration resistance to prevent ozone leakage while simplifying maintenance.
4) AI-based Lamp Output Control System (Option)
This system utilizes edge computing and machine learning to automatically optimize lamp output, maintaining stable water quality while reducing energy consumption.
