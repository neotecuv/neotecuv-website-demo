Technical Series: NAOP

Industrial Water

(NAOP Series)

Download Product Catalog
The Neotec UV Industrial AOP System uses medium-pressure UV lamps and advanced oxidation to break down persistent organic and chemical contaminants in industrial wastewater, ensuring advanced purification for discharge compliance or reuse.

The skid-type configuration integrates key treatment components into a compact unit, allowing for easier installation, operation, and maintenance.

Key Features

The Neotec UV Industrial Wastewater AOP System is designed for flexible operation and straightforward maintenance across a range of industrial wastewater applications.
‍
• Compact skid-mounted design for space-efficient installation
• Medium-pressure UV lamps with multiple power configurations
• Automatic operation with adjustable settings based on influent conditions
• Optional mechanical wiper and chemical cleaning systems for quartz sleeve maintenance

Photo-Fenton Process

The Fenton process is mainly used to treat industrial wastewater containing organic pollutants that are difficult to remove through conventional biological treatment.
Traditional Fenton
• Slow reaction rate
• Large installation footprint required
• Sludge generation due to excess ferric salts, leading to increased sludge disposal costs
UV-Fenton AOP
• Higher organic matter removal efficiency
• Reduced sludge generation due to lower catalyst dosage
• Reduced required ferric salt dosage
