Municipal Wastewater Treatment

Over 70% of Korea’s wastewater treatment facilities utilize ultraviolet disinfection as a safe alternative to conventional chlorination. UV systems eliminate pathogens without generating harmful disinfection byproducts (DBPs), making them suitable for applications ranging from small decentralized facilities to large municipal treatment plants. UV technology is widely adopted for wastewater discharge compliance, water reuse, and reclaimed water production.

Drinking Water Treatment

UV disinfection provides reliable protection against chlorine-resistant pathogens such as Cryptosporidium and Giardia without affecting water taste, odor, or chemistry. Unlike conventional chemical disinfection, UV performance remains stable across varying water temperatures and pH conditions while significantly reducing chlorine demand.

Swimming Pools, Spas, and Recreational Water

Swimming pools, spas, and public bathing facilities rely on chlorine for residual protection, but excessive chlorination can produce chloramines responsible for odor, skin irritation, and facility deterioration. UV treatment significantly reduces chlorine consumption while delivering superior microbial control, creating a cleaner, more comfortable user environment.

Aquaculture, Zoos, and Aquariums

Animal care and aquatic environments face ongoing challenges related to pathogens, algae growth, organic waste, and water quality deterioration. UV treatment provides effective microbial control without introducing toxic chemicals, helping maintain healthier ecosystems, improved water clarity, and safer habitats for aquatic and terrestrial species.

UV Advanced Oxidation (UV-AOP)

UV technology can be combined with oxidants such as hydrogen peroxide to create powerful advanced oxidation processes (AOP). UV-AOP systems effectively degrade emerging contaminants, carcinogenic compounds such as NDMA, persistent organic pollutants, and odor-causing substances, supporting advanced environmental treatment requirements.

UV-AOP is also used to treat industrial wastewater for contaminant removal.
