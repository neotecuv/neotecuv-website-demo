The Neotec UV NAOP Series offers medium-pressure (MP) and low-pressure high-output (LPHO) UV-AOP systems for drinking water treatment and water reuse applications. Selected models have undergone independent third-party UV disinfection validation.

Quartz sleeves are maintained using a mechanical wiper system, with an optional Bubble Clean system that uses microbubbles and water circulation.

Advanced Oxidation Process

The UV-based Advanced Oxidation Process (UV-AOP) combines ultraviolet light with oxidants such as hydrogen peroxide (H₂O₂) to generate highly reactive hydroxyl radicals (•OH). These radicals break down persistent organic contaminants that are difficult to remove by conventional water treatment processes.

UV-AOP can be applied to the treatment of persistent organic contaminants, including 1,4-dioxane, NDMA, pesticides, herbicides, and certain cyanotoxins.
How UV-AOP Works
1. Oxidants such as hydrogen peroxide are added to the water.
2.  Hydroxyl radicals are generated from UV irradiation activating hydrogen peroxide.
3. Hydroxyl radicals react with organic contaminants to break them down.

UV-AOP System Diagram
Click parts of a UV AOP system to learn more.
1. Oxidant Storage Tank stores oxidants such as hydrogen peroxide (H₂O₂) for use in the advanced oxidation process.
2. Oxidant Dosing Control Panel controls the oxidant dosing pump and regulates the amount of oxidant injected into the treatment process through the SCP.
3. Power Supply Panel (PSP) supplies power to the UV lamps through electronic ballasts.

The ballasts automatically regulate lamp output according to control signals from the SCP.
4. System Control Panel (SCP) monitors system operating conditions and automatically adjusts UV lamp output and oxidant dosage in response to changes in flow rate and water quality.
5. Flow Meter measures the real-time flow rate of water and transmits the data to the SCP.

The system uses this information to adjust UV lamp output and oxidant dosage according to changing flow conditions.
6. Oxidant Dosing System (Optional) injects oxidants into the water pipeline through a static mixer or diffuser to ensure proper distribution before the water enters the UV chamber.
7. UV Chamber houses the UV lamp modules and provides the treatment space where UV irradiation and oxidation take place.

Constructed from stainless steel 316L, the chamber can be equipped with optional wiper systems, temperature sensors, and UV intensity sensors.
